# Learn French · Le français facile

Easy French for English speakers, from your first sound to real conversations.
One set of content powers two outputs: an interactive web app and a printable book.

**Live site: https://learnfrench.zyeemzia.com/**

## Open it

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Web app | https://learnfrench.zyeemzia.com/ (installs an offline cache on first visit) |
| Printable book | Open `book.html` → Ctrl + P → Save as PDF / print (A4, background graphics on) |
| Ready PDF | `Learn-French-Book.pdf` |

## What's inside

6 units · 58 lessons · 390 quiz questions · 414 vocabulary words · 98 word-by-word sentences · 36 conversations

1. **Sounds & Letters**: Hear, say and read French with confidence
2. **Writing & Spelling**: Write French neatly, with accents and confidence
3. **First Words & Phrases**: Greetings, numbers, time, family, food and everyday life
4. **Grammar Made Easy**: The patterns behind French, explained simply
5. **Everyday Conversations**: Real French for cafés, shops, travel, health, the phone and friends
6. **Reading & Culture**: Stories, songs, proverbs and life in the French-speaking world

**Web app extras:** built-in natural French voices (female and male) for every word and sentence, so pronunciation works on every device with nothing to install,
a writing studio (watch-the-pen demos and tracing pads with star scores, dictation),
daily missions with XP and streaks, word-by-word sentences with a memorise mode, conversations with play-all and role-play,
Letter Hunt, Listen & Pick, Word Match, Sentence Builder and Speed Quiz games, word families,
spaced-repetition flashcards, a downloadable certificate, a sound setup guide for every device,
light and dark theme and adjustable French text size.

**Book extras:** every lesson with copybook writing pages,
word-search and sentence puzzles, word families, a journal, answer keys, a progress tracker and a certificate.

Fonts are bundled in `fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License.
